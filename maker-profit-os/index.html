Most makers price by feel. Then the platform takes its cut, postage costs more than you charged, and at tax time the numbers are spread across a shoebox, a bank app and three marketplaces. Set it up in one afternoon. Keep it current in about 15 minutes a week.

What it does for you

Materials-to-product costing

Type in what you paid for a 10 lb bag of wax or a case of 24 jars. Materials works out the unit cost, Recipes adds up each product, and Pricing uses the result. Change a supplier price once and every product that uses it updates.

A pricing calculator that tells you when you're underpricing

Suggested price = (full cost + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − fee % − target margin), rounded up to .99. Full cost includes materials, other direct costs, your time at your hourly rate and overhead. Put in your current price and the sheet shows profit per sale, your margin and a clear flag if a product is losing money.

Platform fees worked out on every sale

Fee presets for 8 channels: Etsy, Shopify, Amazon Handmade, your own website (Stripe), craft fair card reader, craft fair cash, wholesale / stockist and Instagram / DM (PayPal), plus spare slots for your own. Defaults are dated November 2026 for US sellers and fully editable, and any single sale can take the exact fee from your statement.

Monthly P&L and a tax set-aside number

See gross sales, fees, postage, expenses by category, net profit and net margin for each month and the year. Choose the Simple (cash) or Product cost (COGS) method in Settings. A set-aside percentage you choose shows how much to move to savings each month. It's a savings habit, not a tax calculation.

A dashboard you can read in 30 seconds

Revenue, net profit, margin, orders, average order value, tax set-aside, top channel and top product, plus 5 charts (6 in Pro and Complete). A data-check strip flags uncategorised expenses and sales with a missing channel or product.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work in Google Sheets and Excel?

Yes, both, plus LibreOffice 7.4 or later. For Google Sheets, upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, then right-click and choose Open with, then Google Sheets. Excel 2019, 2021 and 365 on Windows and Mac open it directly. Apple Numbers is not supported.

Do I need to sell on Etsy?

No. It has presets for 8 sales channels, including Shopify, craft fairs, wholesale and Instagram, and you can add your own.

What if Etsy changes its fees?

Fees are editable in Settings, and any sale can use a fee override from your statement. The defaults are dated November 2026.

Does it import my Etsy or Shopify orders automatically?

No. There is no bank feed or automatic import. You log orders in the Sales tab, either one by one or by pasting values column by column. Logging once a week takes most small shops about 15 minutes.

Do I need to know formulas?

No. White cells are for typing, green cells calculate on their own. Formula cells are locked so you can't break them by accident.

What's the difference between the DEMO and BLANK files?

DEMO is a fictional candle and ceramics shop with a full year of sales, expenses and products, so you can click around and see every tab working. BLANK has the same structure with empty logs and sensible defaults. Explore DEMO, then run your business in BLANK.

What does "Simple (cash)" vs "Product cost (COGS)" mean?

Simple (cash) counts material purchases as expenses when you buy them. Product cost (COGS) counts the materials in each item when it sells. You pick one in Settings and the Monthly P&L follows. Your accountant can confirm which suits you.

Does it handle sales tax or VAT?

Pro and Complete invoices have a sales tax / VAT % line. The workbook does not prepare statutory VAT or sales-tax returns, so it is not a fit for VAT-registered businesses that need them.

Is it halal? Does it include interest?

It is built riba-free. There are no interest, loan, credit-card or late-fee calculations anywhere, including invoices. Pro and Complete include an optional Business Zakat estimator, labelled as an estimate to confirm with a qualified scholar.

Is this tax or accounting advice?

No. It is an organiser and calculator. Check your tax position with a qualified professional.
