Etsy Fees Explained: What You Really Keep per Sale

On a $24 candle with $5.95 shipping, Etsy's standard US fees come to $3.30, or 11% of the order. Here's where every cent goes, what changes the total, and how to price so the fees come out of the buyer's price rather than your profit.

Etsy's fees aren't one fee. They're three or four separate charges, calculated on slightly different amounts, and a couple of optional ones that can more than double the total on a single order. If you price with a vague "Etsy takes about 10%", you'll usually be close and occasionally very wrong.

This guide breaks down each fee for a US seller, works through real orders to the cent, and shows how to build fees into your price.

A note on the figures

Fee figures here are the US defaults we use in our calculators, as of November 2026: 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 payment processing, and $0.20 per listing. Etsy changes fees from time to time, and they differ by country. Always check Etsy's own Fees & Payments Policy and your monthly statement. Not affiliated with Etsy, Inc.

Three things trip sellers up:

  1. The transaction fee applies to shipping too. If you charge $5.95 for shipping, Etsy takes 6.5% of that $5.95 as well.
  2. The listing fee recurs. A listing expires and renews every four months, and when you sell one item from a listing with more stock, the $0.20 renewal is charged again for the next one. In practice, think of it as $0.20 per item sold.
  3. Processing has a fixed part. The $0.25 per order hurts small orders most. On a $5 sticker it's 5% on its own.

Worked example: one candle, one order

A buyer orders one $24.00 candle and pays $5.95 shipping. The order total is $29.95.

FeeCalculationAmount
Transaction fee6.5% × $29.95$1.95
Payment processing3% × $29.95 + $0.25$1.15
Listing renewal$0.20 × 1 item$0.20
Total Etsy fees$3.30

That's 11.0% of the order. You receive $26.65 before postage. If the shipping label costs you $5.99, you're left with $20.66 to cover the candle's full cost and your profit.

What changes the total

Free shipping

If you offer "free" shipping and raise the item price to $29.95 instead, the fees are identical: $3.30. Etsy charges on the order total either way. Free shipping is a presentation choice, not a fee saving. What matters is that the postage cost is inside your price. See the pricing mistakes guide for what happens when it isn't.

Several items in one order

Three candles at $24 with $7.95 shipping: order total $79.95.

FeeCalculationAmount
Transaction6.5% × $79.95$5.20
Processing3% × $79.95 + $0.25$2.65
Listing renewals$0.20 × 3 items$0.60
Total$8.45

That's 10.6% of the order, slightly lower than the single-candle order because the $0.25 is spread across more sales. Bundles and sets are easier on fees.

Discounts and sale events

Fees are charged on what the buyer actually pays. A 20% coupon on the $24 candle brings the order to $25.15 and the fees to $2.84. That looks like a saving of $0.46. But you gave away $4.80 of price to save $0.46 of fees. Always check a discount against your full cost, not just the fee line.

Offsite Ads

When a sale is attributed to one of Etsy's Offsite Ads, an extra fee applies: 15% for shops under the sales threshold that can opt out, and 12% for larger shops that must take part, with a cap per order. On the same $29.95 order, a 15% Offsite Ads fee adds $4.49, taking total fees to $7.79, or 26% of the order.

You can't predict which sales will come from an ad, so treat this as an occasional extra cost. If you're in the programme, look at last year's statements to see what share of your sales it hit, and make sure your margin can absorb it on those orders.

Other fees you may see

Depending on your country and setup, your statement can also show currency conversion fees (if your listing currency differs from your bank account's), a regulatory operating fee in some countries, Etsy Ads you've chosen to run, shipping labels bought through Etsy, and sales tax Etsy collects and remits for you in many US states. Record the fees in your books from the statement, not from memory. Our bookkeeping guide for Etsy sellers shows how.

How to price so fees don't eat your profit

The trap is to work out your cost, add a profit, and then lose 9.5% plus $0.45 to fees. Put fees into the price from the start with this formula:

Price = (full cost + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − fee % − target margin)

For Etsy: fee % = 0.095 and fixed fees = $0.45 per single-item order.

For a candle with a full cost of $10.40 and a 35% target margin: ($10.40 + $0.45) ÷ (1 − 0.095 − 0.35) = $19.55, rounded up to $19.99. At that price Etsy's fees are $2.35 and you keep $7.24, a 36.2% margin. At $12 the same candle keeps one cent. The full worked example is in the candle pricing formula.

Leave shipping out of this formula as long as you charge the buyer roughly what the label costs you. The 9.5% on shipping is small; on the $5.95 above it's $0.57. If you offer free shipping, add the postage to full cost before you run the formula.

Checking fees against your statement

A calculator gives you the expected fee. Your Etsy monthly statement gives you the real one. Once a month:

  • Download the monthly statement from your shop's Finances section
  • Compare total fees with what your sales log expected
  • Look for Offsite Ads fees, currency conversion and any fee you didn't plan for
  • Record Etsy Ads and subscriptions as expenses (they aren't tied to one sale)
  • If a sale's fee differs, override it with the statement figure

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from a $20 sale?

With the US defaults and no shipping or ads: 6.5% ($1.30) + 3% ($0.60) + $0.25 + $0.20 = $2.35, or 11.75%. Add 9.5% of any shipping you charge.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The transaction fee and payment processing are both charged on the order total, which includes the shipping the buyer pays.

Are Etsy fees tax-deductible?

Platform fees are generally treated as a business expense, but rules depend on your country. Keep your monthly statements and ask a qualified accountant.

Why doesn't my calculator match my statement exactly?

Rounding, Offsite Ads, currency conversion, sales tax handling and refunds can all move the real figure. Use the calculator to price and the statement for your books.
