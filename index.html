The problem

Three numbers most small shops can't answer

What does one item really cost?

Not just materials. Your time, a share of overheads, the box it ships in. Our worked candle example has $4.45 of materials and a full cost of $10.40.

What do the fees take?

On a $12 Etsy sale, transaction, processing and listing fees come to $1.59 at the US defaults. Sell that $10.40 candle for $12 and you keep one cent.

What did you actually make this month?

Sales minus fees, postage, materials and every other expense, with a figure to put aside for tax. In one place, not across three apps and a shoebox.

Is the pricing calculator really free?

Yes. Enter $0 at checkout on Gumroad. If you'd like to support the shop, the suggested amount is $3, but the file is the same either way. Gumroad asks for an email address to deliver every download, free or paid.

Does it work in Google Sheets and Excel?

Yes, both, plus LibreOffice. For Google Sheets, upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Sheets. Apple Numbers is not supported.

Do I need to sell on Etsy?

No. There are fee presets for 8 sales channels, including Shopify, Amazon Handmade, your own website, craft fairs, wholesale and Instagram, and you can add your own.

What if Etsy changes its fees?

Fees are editable in Settings, and any single sale can use the exact fee from your statement. The defaults are dated November 2026 for US sellers.

Does it work in my currency or country?

Yes, with any currency label. Tax is a set-aside percentage you choose, not a country-specific tax calculation.

Is this tax or accounting advice?

No. The spreadsheets are an organiser and calculator, and the guides are general information. Check your tax position with a qualified professional.
