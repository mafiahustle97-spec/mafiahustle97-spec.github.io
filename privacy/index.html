Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

This policy covers the Quiet Ledger Studio website (this site). It's short because the site collects very little.

What this site collects

  • No cookies. This site does not set cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts. We don't run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, heatmaps or similar tools here.
  • No forms. There's nothing to sign up for on this site. The quick pricing calculator runs entirely in your browser, and the numbers you type are never sent anywhere.
  • No third-party fonts or embeds. Fonts and images are served from this site itself.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and to keep the service running. GitHub handles that data under its own privacy statement. We don't receive it in a form that identifies you.

Purchases and downloads

When you click a button to get the free calculator or buy Maker Profit OS, you go to Gumroad, which handles checkout, payment and file delivery. Gumroad asks for an email address to deliver every download, free or paid. Gumroad's privacy policy applies to anything you share there. As the seller, we can see the details Gumroad shares with sellers about an order (such as your email address and what you bought) and use them only to deliver your files, provide file support and, if you haven't unsubscribed, send occasional shop emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Links to Gumroad carry simple campaign tags (for example utm_source=quietledger) so we can see which page a visit came from in Gumroad's sales reports. They don't identify you.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from shop emails at any time from any email's footer.
  • To ask what order details we hold, or to have them deleted where we're able to, reply to any Gumroad receipt or email from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
