What the calculator does

  • Costs your materials properly. Enter what you paid and how much you got (a 4,536 g bag of wax for $32, say). It works out the cost per gram, ml, piece or sheet, then multiplies by what one item uses.
  • Counts your time. Minutes per item at your hourly rate. If your time is free, the price is wrong.
  • Adds overhead. A percentage for electricity, tools, the studio corner and everything else you can't pin on one item.
  • Takes fees out of the buyer's price, not your pocket. Percentage fees and fixed per-order fees come off before your margin.
  • Gives a verdict on your current price. Losing money, Under target or On target.
  • Suggests wholesale and retail prices using the common 2×2 rule.

The pricing formula, step by step

  1. Materials: for each material, purchase price ÷ purchase quantity × amount used per item.
  2. Other direct costs: per-item extras such as a gift box or care card.
  3. Labour: minutes per item ÷ 60 × your hourly rate.
  4. Overhead: (materials + other direct + labour) × overhead %.
  5. Full cost = the four layers added up.
  6. Suggested price = (full cost + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − fee % − target margin), rounded up to .99.

The full reasoning, with a second worked example, is in How to price handmade products.

Is it really free?

Yes. Enter $0 at checkout. If you'd like to support the shop, the suggested amount is $3, but the file is the same either way.

Does it work in Google Sheets and Excel?

Yes, both, plus LibreOffice. Apple Numbers is not supported.

Do I need to sell on Etsy?

No. It covers 8 sales channels, including Shopify, craft fairs, wholesale and Instagram, and the fee table is editable.

Does it work for my craft?

If you make a physical product from materials you buy, yes: candles, soap, jewellery, ceramics, stickers, prints, sewing, crochet and more. Units include grams, ml, ounces, pieces, sheets and metres.

Why is the suggested price higher than I expected?

Because it counts your time and overhead, not only materials, and it takes platform fees out before your margin. If the number looks high, check your labour minutes and hourly rate first, then compare channels on the Fee Compare tab.

What does "Lower margin" mean?

Your fee % plus target margin is close to 100%, so no price can hit it. Lower the target margin and the suggested price will appear.

How many products can I price?

No. It is a calculator. The price it suggests depends on the numbers you enter.
